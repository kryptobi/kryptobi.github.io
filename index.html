About me

I build backends – mostly with C# and ASP.NET Core, mostly for web APIs. Starting November 2026 I'm leading the C# .NET backend team at RZH (Rechenzentrum für Heilberufe). Before that I spent seven years at traperto GmbH in Kleve, the last few of them as team lead – working on architecture, code reviews and the question of how a team can do its best work.

I started out in automotive infotainment in Ingolstadt, then moved back to the Lower Rhine. Along the way I picked up C, C++, Java, PHP and JavaScript – but .NET is where I stuck.

I care more about readable code than clever code. And Git is a bit of a hobby horse of mine: if someone on the team is stuck in the middle of a rebase, they're welcome to drop by.
